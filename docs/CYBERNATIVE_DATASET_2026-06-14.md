# CyberNative DPO dataset — inspection + v8.4 train signal (2026-06-14)

Question from Zakhar: isn't there a suitable dataset on HF? Did a fresh targeted
search (we hadn't, with today's precise requirement). Most HF vuln datasets do
NOT help — they fix label noise (CleanVul) or are function-level C/C++ (PrimeVul,
DiverseVul), neither of which is our problem (snippet-sufficiency for web). One
candidate fits the shape: **CyberNative/Code_Vulnerability_Security_DPO**.

## What it is

- 4656 synthetic vuln/safe pairs (DPO: `rejected`=vulnerable, `chosen`=fixed),
  11 languages ~423 each, generated by deepseek-coder-33b. Apache 2.0.
- **243 XSS pairs** (169 JS, 21 PHP, 20 Ruby, ...), ~1700 web-lang pairs total
  across XSS/SQLi/injection/IDOR/deserialization.
- Self-contained, snippet-sufficient: source + sink in one snippet, escaping is
  the discriminator. This is the structural property our CVEFixes/CrossVul pile
  lacks (69% of TP rows had the sink out of window).

## Quality: mixed but the core signal is clean

Bad:
- **9% of XSS pairs have generation garbage** (CJK/non-ASCII tokens injected into
  code, e.g. `appendChild(element)妆;`) — unparseable. Regex-filterable.
- **29% of XSS use encodeURIComponent as the "fix"** — wrong tool for HTML
  context; a misconception if learned.

But the decisive test says usable:
- GPT-5.3 pair discrimination on 18 XSS pairs = **17/18 = 94%** (vs 100% on our
  hand-built, 44% on real mined CrossVul). The "raw output = vuln, processed =
  safe" signal is consistently present and frontier-confirmable — exactly what
  v8.3.1 is blind to (killer test 0/20).

## Filtered v8.4 training signal (built)

`scripts/build_cybernative_train.py --cwes CWE-79 --drop-encodeuri --with-reasoning`
→ `data/train/cwe_web_cybernative/train.json`:
- **150 clean XSS pairs / 300 cases** after dropping 22 garbage + 69 weak-fix
  (encodeURIComponent) + 2 dup. Quality over quantity.
- assistant target carries a short code-grounded reasoning naming the escaping
  mechanism (not NVD cargo-cult boilerplate).

## Experiment design (v8.4-CWE79-exp1)

- TRAIN: filtered CyberNative XSS (150 pairs), continue-train FROM the v8.3.1
  adapter (NOT from base) — preserves v8.3.1's general triage behavior, adds the
  missing escaping discrimination. Old snippet-insufficient pile is NOT re-fed.
- TEST (held out, disjoint sources):
  1. hand-built 20 pairs (frontier-anchored 20/20) — synthetic generalization
  2. mined real CrossVul pairs — REAL-code transfer (the verdict that matters)
  3. gold-core (n=71) — did general behavior survive (forgetting check)
- METRIC: pair discrimination, frontier-anchored. Baseline v8.3.1 = 0/20.

## Honest prediction

- Controlled/synthetic discrimination climbs from 0/20: HIGH confidence (any
  exposure to the escaping signal moves it off the floor).
- Transfer to real mined pairs: MODERATE confidence — this is the real result.
- Forgetting risk on gold-core: mitigated by continue-training from v8.3.1 +
  small dose; watch the gold-core number.

## On reusing old datasets

No re-feeding the old pile (it taught the blindness). Old-model knowledge enters
via the v8.3.1 starting weights. A small clean general-triage anchor (the 31%
learnable subset) is held in reserve, added only if forgetting appears.
