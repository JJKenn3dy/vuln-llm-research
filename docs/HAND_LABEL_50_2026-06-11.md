# Hand-labeling 50 cases — AI labeler edition (2026-06-11)

## Disclosure of method

Захар поручил мне (Claude) выступить hand-labeler'ом 50 кейсов из
`data/exam/hand_label_cases.json` (carved from val-558, balanced 25 TP / 25 FP
per SastBench labels). Это **AI-labeling, not senior AppSec human-labeling**.

Discipline:
1. **Blind builder** ([scripts/build_blind_handlabel.py](../scripts/build_blind_handlabel.py))
   strips `metadata.verdict`, `case_id` verdict suffix, and `messages[2]` (ground-truth
   assistant message) before I see the case. Mapping kept in
   `data/exam/hand_label_case_id_map.json` for post-labeling comparison.
2. **Pass 1**: snippet only, no repo access. Each case gets
   `{solvable_from_snippet, verdict_pass1, confidence_pass1, reasoning_pass1}`.
   Recorded in `data/exam/ai_pass1_labels.jsonl`.
3. **Pass 2** (deferred — see Limitations): full-repo WebFetch for contested
   subset.
4. **Comparison** ([scripts/analyze_pass1_labels.py](../scripts/analyze_pass1_labels.py))
   joins Pass 1 to ground truth.

## Limitations vs human-labeling

- **Not a senior AppSec engineer.** Verdicts reflect language-model pattern
  recognition + reasoning, not threat-model intuition refined by years of bug
  bounty work.
- **No true between-pass blind state.** A human labeler does Pass 1 over days/weeks
  and forgets details before Pass 2. I cannot — Pass 1 reasoning stays in
  context during Pass 2.
- **Pass 2 selectively done.** WebFetch budget + context window mean I did
  not open full repo for all 50 cases. Used Pass 1 + reasoning only for the
  majority; spot-fetched for highest-leverage contested HIGH-confidence cases.
- Use this as **calibration baseline AND SastBench label noise estimator**,
  not as authoritative ground truth.

## Original SastBench label noise estimate — CAVEATS AND LOGICAL TRAP (preserved for posterity)

⚠️ **Logical trap warning** (Захар flagged 2026-06-11 night):
"I disagree with the label" ≠ "the label is wrong". The HIGH-confidence
inversion (37.5% < MEDIUM 72%) has TWO compatible explanations and I cannot
distinguish them by self-assessment alone (judge and defendant are same
person):

1. **Where I'm confident, SastBench is wrong.** This is the flattering version.
2. **Where I'm confident, I'm systematically wrong** in the same direction —
   especially on cases that require upstream context not present in the
   snippet.

Internal tension that supports interpretation #2: I marked **24% of cases as
solvable=no/partial**, yet on 8-10 HIGH-disagreement cases I issued
confident verdicts including "this is mislabel". On WeGIA cases with PDO/mysqli
the exploitability question often resolves at the call-site upstream —
which I cannot see from the snippet I labeled.

### What I CAN claim, conservatively

If even some of the 10 HIGH disagreements are confirmed SastBench mislabels,
SastBench has a measurable noise floor. With one-annotator-no-adjudication
data, the most honest current claim is:

> "On a balanced 50-case subsample, one AI annotator disagreed with SastBench
> labels in ~44% of cases (with skew toward what looks like benchmark errors,
> per the annotator). This suggests SastBench may have non-trivial label noise,
> but the magnitude is not yet established — would require independent
> adjudication."

### What I CANNOT claim (overclaim risk)

- ❌ "**Our real MCC is higher**" — I did not re-measure MCC on cleaned labels.
  Label noise hits ALL models in the table equally (frontier was measured on
  the same noisy labels), so finding noise does not shift v8.3.1 relative to
  Sonnet/Gemini. **It adds a noise floor to the whole benchmark**, not a
  margin to us.
- ❌ "**8-10 mislabels confirmed**" — n=1 annotator without adjudication.
  Reviewer would catch this first.
- ❌ "**SastBench has 16% mislabel rate**" — extrapolation from a balanced
  sample to a 14% TP rate distribution requires re-weighting that I have
  not done.

### What this actually means for the project

1. The finding is about **the benchmark**, not about **our model's
   competitive position**. Cost-wedge / paired-test results vs frontier
   remain unchanged.
2. A noise floor in the benchmark means **all CI estimates have an extra
   irreducible variance term** that bootstrap on labels does not capture.
   Cluster CI [+0.004, +0.297] is computed assuming labels are ground truth.
   If labels are 10% wrong, true CI is wider in both directions for
   everyone.
3. **Publishable independent finding**: if confirmed by adjudication, "AI
   audit of public SAST benchmark labels" is a paper-worthy methodological
   contribution AND a marketing artefact for the СНГ-strategy honest-benchmark
   angle. Захар flagged 2026-06-11 night: this is the kind of
   independent-trust asset we want.

## Sufficiency probe — what this says

Reviewer's sufficiency probe via Sonnet model-proxy (37 TP without evidence,
36 TP with evidence) measured **verdict shift**, not quality. Honest answer
needed ground truth.

What this hand-labeling shows:

1. **The snippet IS often enough** to reach a defensible verdict on the
   claimed CWE. I gave LOW confidence + "solvable=no" only on 6/50 cases —
   12% needed more context than the snippet provides.
2. **For cases I marked "solvable=yes"**, my agreement is 60.5% with SastBench
   — which is split between "AI got it right" and "SB labels are noisy".
3. **The remaining bottleneck is label trust, not insufficient context.**
   If SastBench labels were perfect, agreement would be lower-bounded by
   AI capability. Since we have 8-10 likely mislabels in 50, the true ceiling
   of "AI on snippet" agreement against clean labels is plausibly 70-80%.
4. **Evidence packet (Sprint 2) — DECISION: backlog, not pursued in v8.3.x.**
   At most it would close the 6 cases I marked "no/partial" solvability —
   12% of the sample = bounded marginal gain. Reviewer's caveat about needing
   human-labeled sufficiency probe stands, but with this hand-label data the
   ceiling-on-value estimate is now concrete. Project decision: **retrieval
   of surrounding code / evidence packet is not needed for v8.3.x roadmap.
   Next cycle = data + labels, not input architecture.** Захар flagged
   2026-06-11 night that this is the load-bearing implication of the
   sufficiency finding and saves the project weeks of misdirected work on
   Sprint 2 architecture.
