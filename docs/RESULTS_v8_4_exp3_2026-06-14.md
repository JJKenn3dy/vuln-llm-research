# v8.4-exp3 (scaled anchor) — quality > quantity; exp2 stays the winner (2026-06-14)

Hypothesis: scaling the real anchor 3x (40 → 151 real pairs, across web CWEs)
would push recall up (exp2's weak spot). It did NOT. The scaled anchor used a
RELAXED filter (no escaping-keyword requirement, window around the largest hunk
across CWE-79/89/78/94/22), which reintroduced the noise exp2's clean filter
removed (pairs where the "safe" half still contains other unescaped sinks).

## Comparison (all continue-trained from v8.3.1)

| model | gold-core MCC | gold DR | gold FAR | mined-heldout MCC | mined DR | mined disc | controlled disc |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v8.3.1 | +0.447 | 64% | 18% | ~0 (paranoid TP) | — | — | 0/20 |
| exp1 (synthetic-only) | +0.313 | 14% | 0% | 0.108 | 15% | 7% | 11/20 |
| **exp2 (clean anchor, 40 pairs)** | **+0.517** | 41% | 2% | **0.255** | **19%** | **16%** | 10/20 |
| exp3 (scaled noisy, 151 pairs) | +0.517 | 41% | 2% | 0.075 | 6% | 6% | 10/20 |

## Verdict

- **exp3 is NOT better.** gold-core identical to exp2 (+0.517); mined-heldout
  WORSE (MCC 0.255→0.075, DR 19%→6%, over-clears 28/31). More noisy anchor made
  real-code recall worse, not better.
- **Lesson: anchor quality > quantity.** exp2's 40 escaping-keyword-filtered,
  well-windowed pairs beat exp3's 151 relaxed-filter pairs. Pairs where the fix
  isn't a clean single escaping (multiple sinks, the "safe" half still looks
  vulnerable) teach the model "vuln-looking code = FP" → recall drops.
- The held-out real test caught it again. Naive scaling would have looked fine
  on gold-core alone (+0.517); the mined-heldout exposed the regression.

## Best model: v8.4-exp2

exp2 remains the project best:
- gold-core MCC +0.517 (> v8.3.1 +0.447), FAR 18%→2%
- escaping-blindness fixed (0/20 → 10/20)
- real-transfer MCC 0.255 (best of all variants)

## What would actually raise recall

More CLEAN real anchor — escaping-keyword-filtered AND GPT-5.3-validated
(discrimination-confirmed), well-windowed, ideally CWE-79-focused. That hits the
fundamental scarcity: clean real snippet-sufficient TPs are rare (the original
root cause). Getting them at volume needs GPT-5.3 validation per pair (~$, time),
not a relaxed regex filter.

## Recommendation

Model-path diminishing returns are now visible: exp2 is a solid, defensible best
(beats v8.3.1 on the trusted metric, escaping fixed, clean FAR), and the next
recall gain requires expensive clean-data curation against a scarcity wall.
Honest move: **freeze exp2 as the new baseline** and shift to demand validation
(the riskiest hypothesis, untouched, needs no compute). One more clean-anchor
curation pass is a defensible later iteration, not a now-blocker.
