## Where we are heading

Commercial product: **GitHub App that triages SAST findings, beating GPT-4o on the
filtering task, $9/dev/month**.

The technical bet is that a fine-tuned 7B specialist, trained on real CVE-derived
TP/FP pairs with project-stratified splits, can deliver BalAcc ≥ 0.65 on triage
while frontier general LLMs stay near 0.50. Today's baseline run confirms the
ceiling.
